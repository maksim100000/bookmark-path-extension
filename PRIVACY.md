# Privacy Policy for Bookmark Search & Path: Simple Viewer

**Effective Date:** June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the use of the **Bookmark Search & Path — Plain Viewer** browser extension (the "Extension") hosted in the repository [https://github.com/maksim100000/bookmark-path-extension](https://github.com/maksim100000/bookmark-path-extension). We take your privacy very seriously and operate on a strict **Privacy First** principle.

## 1. No Data Collection
The Extension does **not** collect, store, log, track, or share any personal data, browsing history, or user activity. No analytics tools or third-party trackers are integrated into the extension.

## 2. Local Operation Only
All operations performed by the Extension happen entirely locally on your device:
* **Bookmarks Access:** The Extension requests permission to read your browser bookmarks solely to provide real-time search functionality and display folder paths within the side panel interface.
* **No External Transmissions:** Your bookmark data, search queries, and folder hierarchies are never transmitted over the internet, never sent to external servers, and never accessible by the developer or any third party.

## 3. Permissions Justification
The Extension requests only the minimal permissions necessary to function:
* `bookmarks`: Required strictly to read and search your local bookmarks and generate their folder structures.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The Extension does not integrate with any third-party analytics, tracking tools, or advertising networks.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Your continued use of the Extension after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the new policy.

## 6. Contact & Support
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue in the official GitHub repository: [https://github.com/maksim100000/bookmark-path-extension/issues](https://github.com/maksim100000/bookmark-path-extension/issues).
